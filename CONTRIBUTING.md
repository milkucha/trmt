# Contributing to The Roads More Travelled (TRMT)

Thank you for your interest in contributing! Please read this document before submitting a pull request.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before your contribution can be merged, you must agree to the
[Harmony Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

In short, it means:
- You keep ownership of your code.
- You grant the project a permanent, irrevocable license to use, modify, and relicense your contribution under any terms, including commercial ones.
- You confirm the code is yours to contribute.

**To accept the CLA**, post the following comment verbatim in your pull request:

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md and I agree to its terms.

Your pull request will not be merged until this comment is present. This comment, tied to your verified GitHub identity, serves as your signature.

## If you do not own all the copyright in your submission

Do not submit the contribution. Contact milkucha@disroot.org first to discuss the situation before opening a PR.

## How to submit a contribution

1. Fork the repository and create a branch from the relevant version branch (e.g. `main`, `1.21.4`).
2. Make your changes.
3. Open a pull request against the appropriate branch.
4. Post the CLA acceptance comment (see above).
5. Wait for review. The project owner may request changes or decline the contribution at their discretion.

## Attribution

Accepted contributors are listed in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) with their name or handle and a brief description of their contribution. This satisfies the attribution requirement of the project's CC BY-NC 4.0 license.
